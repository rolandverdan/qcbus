QC Bus Tracker

Track Your Ride in Real-Time!

The official tracking app for Quezon City free buses. Get live map updates, instant arrival notifications, and save your daily routes.

Download for Android

Requires Android 8.0 or later.

Live Map Tracking

See exactly where your bus is on the map and never guess when it will arrive at your stop again.

Arrival Alerts

Get push notifications when your bus is approaching so you can time your walk to the terminal perfectly.

Saved Routes

Bookmark your daily commutes to get instant updates on delays, capacity, and active buses on your route.

Install on iPhone

Apple requires a manual installation through Safari.

1

Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the Safari menu.

2

Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
