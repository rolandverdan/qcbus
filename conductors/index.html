QCommute

Quezon City Conductor Portal

Version 1.0.0

QCommute Conductor Portal enables bus conductors to manage line trips, track boardings and alightings in real time via manual counter or AI doorway scanner, broadcast emergency SOS alerts, and provide live occupancy updates to Quezon City commuters.

Conductor Operational Guide

Trip Management: Select your route direction and tap "Start Trip". Tap "End Trip" upon completing the line run to record official trip statistics.
Passenger Counting: Use the large + / - buttons on the Counter tab, or use the Scanner tab for AI-assisted overhead doorway detection.
Emergency SOS: Tap the SOS button to immediately alert QC Command Center for medical, accident, crime, or breakdown incidents.
